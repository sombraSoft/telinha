Telinha is a self-hosted screen share for a Discord group. Members log in with
Discord, a role check decides who gets in, and the `/telinha` command opens a
room at `/r/<code>` and posts a live card in the allowed channels. The video
goes through a LiveKit SFU running next to it. It exists because Discord Go
Live is blocked in Brazil.

You need a Discord server you administer, a machine that stays on, and an
HTTPS address: at home a domain on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Tunnel) or a free
DuckDNS name on port 8443; on a VPS your own domain, DuckDNS or sslip.io. At
home you also forward the two media ports (and 8443 with DuckDNS) on your
router, or let UPnP do it.

Everything is one program configured by one file, `telinha.env`. The setup
wizard writes it for you, downloads the helper programs Telinha needs and sets
it up as a service.
